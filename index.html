FAQ

Questions? We've got sweet answers.

Everything you need to know about ordering, delivery, customization and pricing at Fari Cake Bake.

How do I place an order?

Simply browse our menu and tap 'Get Quote' or 'WhatsApp' on any item. Your selection is prefilled and our team confirms details, pricing and timing on WhatsApp.

How much notice do you need?

For regular items 24-48 hours is enough. Custom or multi-tier cakes are best ordered 3-5 days ahead.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes, we deliver across the city. Delivery is free on orders above Rs. 3,000.

Can I fully customise my cake?

Absolutely — flavours, colours, size and design can all be tailored to your event.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing depends on size, flavour and design complexity. Share your idea and we'll send an exact quote.
